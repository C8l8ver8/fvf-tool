MAD = mean absolute difference between A and B over all pixels and colours (0 = identical, 255 = black vs white). Bias = average (B − A); a negative green bias means B is darker, which shows as a pink/purple cast.

You can open STTNG.ZIP directly; you will be asked which uncompressed .fvf inside it to load. Audio plays from file A. The pictures are decoded exactly as the game's decoder does it (320×200, 15 fps).
